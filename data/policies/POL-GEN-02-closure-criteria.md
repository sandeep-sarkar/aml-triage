# POL-GEN-02: Closing alerts with no typology match

## Summary

An alert may be closed with no escalation where transaction activity is
consistent with the customer's known profile and no specific typology
indicator (structuring, rapid movement of funds, high-risk jurisdiction
exposure, etc.) is present.

## Required action

The disposition must state which typologies were considered and briefly
note why each was not triggered. A bare "no issues found" is not
sufficient documentation.

## Escalation override

Regardless of this policy, any transaction individually exceeding
£10,000 in cash, or any counterparty appearing on an internal watchlist,
requires escalation under separate policy (POL-WATCH-03, not covered
here) even if no typology pattern is present.
